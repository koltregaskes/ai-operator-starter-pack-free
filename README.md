# AI Operator Starter Pack — Free Edition

> Three lessons, four exercises, one reference card, ten prompts.
> For people who want to run AI tools properly, not just use them.

## Why a free edition?

Three reasons.

1. **Operator-grade material grows by recommendation, not by megaphone.** Open-sourcing the worksheet and prompt kit means you can use them, share them, and adapt them — and the people who get the most out of them are the people most likely to recommend the paid pack.
2. **Credibility.** We're not gating valuable content. We're charging for the typesetting, design, packaging, and the small joy of having it as one finished object.
3. **Future-proofing.** When the paid pack is updated, this repo gets updated too. Forks stay accurate.

If you find this useful, the kindest thing you can do is send the link to **one** other person who you think would also benefit.

## Contributing

This repo is open to issues and PRs that:

- Fix typos in the markdown
- Add specific real-world examples in the worksheet
- Suggest additional operator prompts (with reasoning) for the kit

It is not open to:

- Wholesale rewrites

The bar is **does this make the operator material more useful for real people doing real work?** If yes, send a PR. If unclear, open an issue first.
