# Welcome — AI Operator Starter Pack

One page. Friendly. Sets expectations for how to use the pack.

---

Hello, and thank you for picking this up.

This is a small pack on purpose. The free edition is four working files in this repository, plus three lessons on AI Resource Hub: about an hour of focused reading and a few hours of practice spread over a working week. There is no certificate at the end and no community to join. Read it, use it, keep the reference card next to your monitor, and come back to it when something feels off with your tools.

**The order I would read these in:**

1. Start with **Lesson 1 — What Is a Harness?** It is the foundation; everything else assumes the vocabulary.
2. Read **Lesson 2 — What Is a Transformer?** for what the model itself actually does.
3. Read **Lesson 3 — Model Layers Explained** to understand why the same model can feel different depending on context.

Then turn to the practical material:

4. Open the **Operator Worksheet** and do **Exercise 1 — The Harness Audit** on a tool you use almost every day. Forty-five minutes. Do this once before reading the other exercises.
5. Pin the **Harness Reference Card** to a wall, a corkboard, or the back of your monitor. Look at it the next time something feels wrong.
6. Skim the **Operator Prompt Kit** the first time. Pick three prompts that match a problem you have right now. Use them this week.

**A small request:** if any single piece of this pack lands particularly well or particularly badly, open an issue on this repository and write one sentence about why. The pack improves faster from one honest "this exercise did not work" than from ten polite thank-you notes.

**Where the rest of this material lives:**

The three lessons in this pack are part of a longer Academy series on **AI Resource Hub** (`airesourcehub.com/academy`). All of it is free to read on the site. The pack exists because some readers wanted a clean printed-or-PDF version they could keep alongside the worksheet, the card, and the prompts — one finished object instead of five browser tabs.

If you find this useful, the kindest thing you can do is send the link to **one** other person who you think would also benefit. Quiet operator-focused material grows by recommendation, not by megaphone.

Thanks for reading.
